How it works

From setup to report in three steps

We bring the equipment, it measures for a week, and we walk you through what it found.

  1. 1

    We set it up

    A noiserisk technician comes to your home and sets up the monitoring equipment so it hears the noise outside. It is connected to power and Wi-Fi, and tested before we leave.

  2. 2

    It monitors for 7 days

    The device runs for a full week and logs noise events, so the results show the difference between day and night, and between weekdays and the weekend. You can follow it in real time on the device dashboard.

  3. 3

    We collect it and walk you through the report

    A noiserisk technician collects the equipment and goes through the report with you. You get it as a PDF and as an online version you can come back to. If you want to analyse the data yourself, you can also get it in JSONL format.

lock Privacy

No sound is stored, and no audio leaves the device

The device analyses audio in 1-second increments and classifies the noise on the device itself. Once a second has been analysed, the audio is discarded. Your report is built from the levels, sound types, and grade, never from recordings.

  • timer Analysed in 1-second increments
